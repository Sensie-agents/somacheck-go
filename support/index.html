Support

Contact us

Email hi@joinsensie.com. Include the app version, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem occurred. Do not email passwords or sensitive health information.

Account and password help

Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen. Confirmation and recovery links expire; request a new link if the newest one no longer works.

Delete your account

In SomaCheck, open Profile and choose “Delete Account.” This removes your account and requests deletion of account-linked SomaCheck data. If you cannot access the app, email support from the address on the account.

Safety

SomaCheck is a general-wellness tool, not emergency or medical care. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent medical help, contact local emergency services.

SomaCheck

World Vibe for Chrome

The extension is not in the Chrome Web Store yet. Until the listing is live, you can install it by hand.

  1. Get the extension folder from the SomaCheck team through support.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and pick the folder.
  4. Highlight any line on a web page and bring it to World Vibe with one click.

Your check-in counts first. A shared line appears on World Vibe after your gesture.
